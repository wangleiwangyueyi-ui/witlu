李白仗剑壮游行迹（701年 — 762年 · 武周长安元年至唐宝应元年）

Li Bai's Grand Travels Across Tang China (701 CE – 762 CE · High Tang Dynasty)

时代跨度：701年 — 762年 · 武周长安元年至唐宝应元年 途经站点：共 12 站 起点 → 终点：碎叶城 → 当涂

全线途经古今地名：碎叶城（今吉尔吉斯斯坦托克马克） → 绵州青莲（今四川江油青莲镇） → 渝州（今重庆） → 荆门（今湖北宜都） → 扬州（今江苏扬州） → 安陆（今湖北安陆） → 长安（今陕西西安） → 洛阳（今河南洛阳） → 睢阳（今河南商丘） → 任城（今山东济宁） → 白帝城（今重庆奉节） → 当涂（今安徽马鞍山·当涂）。

Route Stops: Suyab (Tokmok, Kyrgyzstan) → Qinglian, Mianzhou (Jiangyou, Sichuan) → Yuzhou (Chongqing) → Jingmen (Yidu, Hubei) → Yangzhou (Yangzhou, Jiangsu) → Anlu (Anlu, Hubei) → Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) → Luoyang (Luoyang, Henan) → Suiyang (Shangqiu, Henan) → Rencheng (Jining, Shandong) → Baidicheng (Fengjie, Chongqing) → Dangtu (Dangtu, Ma'anshan, Anhui).

李白仗剑壮游行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（12 站）

第 1 站 碎叶城（今吉尔吉斯斯坦托克马克）· 诞于西域·安西碎叶 Suyab (Tokmok, Kyrgyzstan) — Birth at Suyab in the Western Regions
701年（长安元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

诞于唐安西都护府碎叶城，幼承丝路胡汉交融之风，五岁随父李客东迁入蜀。

Born at Suyab in the Anxi Protectorate on the Silk Road frontier, Li Bai migrated east with his family to Sichuan at age five.

范传正《唐左拾遗翰林学士李公新墓碑》：“隋末多难，一房被窜于碎叶，流离散落，隐易姓名。”Fan Chuanzheng, New Tomb Inscription for Li Bai: "Amid the turmoil at the end of Sui, his branch of the clan was exiled to Suyab, living incognito until returning to Shu in the Shenlong era."
第 2 站 绵州青莲（今四川江油青莲镇）· 卜居青莲·大匡山读书 Qinglian, Mianzhou (Jiangyou, Sichuan) — Youth in Qinglian & Studying on Mount Dakuang
705年（神龙元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

客居绵州青莲乡（号“青莲居士”），隐大匡山读书习剑，游峨眉获司马承祯赞“仙风道骨”。

Growing up in Qinglian, Mianzhou, young Li Bai studied on Mount Dakuang, practiced swordsmanship, and was praised as having “immortal bones.”

李白《上安州裴长史书》：“五岁诵六甲，十岁观百家……十五观奇书，作赋凌相如。”Li Bai, Letter to Administrator Pei of Anzhou: "At five I recited the calendar classics; at ten I read the Hundred Schools; at fifteen I composed rhapsodies rivaling Sima Xiangru."
第 3 站 渝州（今重庆）· 夜发清溪·思君下渝州 Yuzhou (Chongqing) — Sailing Down the Min & Yangtze Rivers to Yuzhou
724年（开元十二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

年二十四辞别蜀中山水，自峨眉清溪乘舟顺岷江、长江东下渝州，赋《峨眉山月歌》。

At twenty-four, Li Bai set out from Mount Emei and sailed down the Min and Yangtze rivers to Yuzhou, composing Moon Over Mount Emei.

李白《峨眉山月歌》：“峨眉山月半轮秋，影入平羌江水流。夜发清溪向三峡，思君不见下渝州。”Li Bai, Moon Over Mount Emei: "A half-wheel of autumn moon above Mount Emei casts its reflection into the flowing Pingqiang River; setting out by night from Qingxi toward the Three Gorges, longing for you unseen as I sail down to Yuzhou."
第 4 站 荆门（今湖北宜都）· 仗剑去国·辞亲出三峡 Jingmen (Yidu, Hubei) — Leaving Shu with Sword in Hand Through the Three Gorges
725年（开元十三年） · 在地图定位此站 →

怀济世志“仗剑去国，辞亲远游”，穿三峡出荆门山初见江汉平原壮阔，赋《渡荆门送别》。

Sailing through the Three Gorges past Jingmen Mountain onto the vast Jianghan Plain, he wrote Farewell at Jingmen.

李白《渡荆门送别》：“山随平野尽，江入大荒流。月下飞天镜，云生结海楼。”Li Bai, Farewell at Jingmen: "Mountains vanish into the level plain; the Great River rolls into the boundless wild. Beneath the moon a celestial mirror flies; rising clouds weave ocean pavilions."
第 5 站 扬州（今江苏扬州）· 漫游吴越·散金三十万 Yangzhou (Yangzhou, Jiangsu) — Grand Tour of Wu-Yue & Spending 300,000 Cash in Yangzhou
726年（开元十四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

顺江东下游庐山、金陵至扬州，豪爽济困“散金三十余万”，写下《望庐山瀑布》《静夜思》。

Sailing down the Yangtze past Mount Lu to prosperous Yangzhou, he gave away 300,000 coins to destitute scholars and wrote Quiet Night Thought.

李白《上安州裴长史书》：“曩昔东游维扬，不逾一年，散金三十余万，有落魄公子，悉皆济之。”Li Bai: "Formerly traveling east to Yangzhou, in less than a year I scattered more than three hundred thousand in gold, coming to the aid of every stranded gentleman."
第 6 站 安陆（今湖北安陆）· 酒隐安陆·登临黄鹤楼 Anlu (Anlu, Hubei) — Ten Years in Anlu & Farewell at Yellow Crane Tower
727年（开元十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

赘于安陆故相许圉师孙女家，“酒隐安陆”十年，频游江夏、襄阳交孟浩然，赋诗黄鹤楼送别。

Marrying into former Chancellor Xu Yushi’s clan in Anlu, he lived there a decade, befriending Meng Haoran and seeing him off at Yellow Crane Tower.

李白《黄鹤楼送孟浩然之广陵》：“故人西辞黄鹤楼，烟花三月下扬州。孤帆远影碧空尽，唯见长江天际流。”Li Bai, Seeing Off Meng Haoran at Yellow Crane Tower: "My old friend bids farewell to the west at Yellow Crane Tower, sailing down to Yangzhou in the misty blossoms of March."
第 7 站 长安（今陕西西安）· 奉诏入京·翰林待诏 Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Summoned to Chang'an — Imperial Hanlin Academy
742年（天宝元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

奉唐玄宗诏西入长安，贺知章读《蜀道难》惊呼“谪仙人”，玄宗降辇步迎、诏供奉翰林。

Summoned to Chang’an by Emperor Xuanzong, Li Bai was hailed by He Zhizhang as a “Banished Immortal” and served in the Hanlin Academy.

李白《南陵别儿童入京》：“仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人！”Li Bai, Parting from My Children at Nanling for the Capital: "Throwing back my head with a great laugh I stride out the gate—how could men like us remain forever amid the mugwort?"
第 8 站 洛阳（今河南洛阳）· 赐金放还·洛阳逢杜甫 Luoyang (Luoyang, Henan) — Leaving Court & Historic Meeting with Du Fu in Luoyang
744年（天宝三载春） · 在地图定位此站 →

遭权贵谗毁获“赐金放还”，东至洛阳与年三十三的杜甫初遇定交，诗史双子星于此际会。

Granted gold to leave court in 744 CE, Li Bai traveled east to Luoyang where he met the younger Du Fu—the twin peaks of Tang poetry.

杜甫《寄李十二白二十韵》：“乞归优诏许，遇我宿心亲。醉舞梁园夜，行歌泗水春。”Du Fu, Twenty Rhymes Sent to Li Bai: "Your plea to retire was granted by gracious edict; meeting me, our kindred hearts grew close—dancing drunk in Liang Garden by night, singing along the Si River in spring."
第 9 站 睢阳（今河南商丘）· 梁园豪游·偕杜甫高适猎孟诸 Suiyang (Shangqiu, Henan) — Roaming Liang-Song with Du Fu & Gao Shi
744年（天宝三载秋） · 在地图定位此站 →

秋与杜甫、高适同游梁宋（开封、商丘），登吹台怀古、纵猎孟诸泽，于梁园题壁作《梁园吟》。

In autumn 744 CE, Li Bai, Du Fu, and Gao Shi roamed the Liang-Song plains, ascending the ancient Liang terrace and hunting at Mengzhu.

李白《梁园吟》：“我浮黄河去京阙，挂席欲进波连山。天长水阔厌远涉，访古始及平台间。”Li Bai, Ballad of Liang Garden: "Floating down the Yellow River away from the palace towers, I hoisted sail across mountain-high waves until reaching the ancient terrace of Liang."
第 10 站 任城（今山东济宁）· 东游齐鲁·梦游天姥 Rencheng (Jining, Shandong) — Sojourn in Qilu & Dreaming of Mount Tianmu
745年（天宝四载） · 在地图定位此站 →

寄家山东任城，与杜甫同游齐鲁、受道箓；临别南游越中时赋《梦游天姥吟留别》抒傲岸之志。

Making his home in Rencheng and roaming Qilu with Du Fu, Li Bai composed Dreaming of Mount Tianmu: A Farewell Song before heading south.

李白《梦游天姥吟留别》：“安能摧眉折腰事权贵，使我不得开心颜！”Li Bai, Dreaming of Mount Tianmu: "How can I bow my brow and bend my waist to serve the powerful, denying myself a joyful heart and open brow?"
第 11 站 白帝城（今重庆奉节）· 长流夜郎·白帝遇赦还江陵 Baidicheng (Fengjie, Chongqing) — Exile to Yelang & Imperial Pardon at Baidicheng
759年（乾元二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

因入永王李璘幕府坐长流夜郎，行至巫山白帝城忽逢大赦，惊喜放舟东下江陵，赋《早发白帝城》。

Banished toward Yelang after Prince Lin of Yong’s defeat, he received an imperial amnesty at Baidicheng and raced downstream to Jiangling.

李白《早发白帝城》：“朝辞白帝彩云间，千里江陵一日还。两岸猿声啼不住，轻舟已过万重山。”Li Bai, Departing Baidicheng at Dawn: "At dawn I leave Baidicheng amid rainbow clouds; a thousand li to Jiangling I return in a single day. While gibbons on both banks cry without cease, my light skiff has already shot past ten thousand mountains."
第 12 站 当涂（今安徽马鞍山·当涂）· 赋临路歌·终老当涂青山 Dangtu (Dangtu, Ma'anshan, Anhui) — Final Song at Dangtu & Resting by Mount Qing
762年（宝应元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

六十一岁请缨从军因病折返，依当涂令李阳冰，宝应元年冬赋《临路歌》绝笔病逝，葬于青山。

After illness cut short his bid to join Li Guangbi’s army at 61, he stayed in Dangtu, composed his final Song Before the Road, and died in 762 CE.

李白《临路歌》：“大鹏飞兮振八裔，中天摧兮力不济。馀风激兮万世，游扶桑兮挂石袂。”Li Bai, Song Before the Road: "The Great Peng soared and shook the eight horizons; broken in mid-heaven, its strength gave out—yet its lingering wind stirs ten thousand generations."
